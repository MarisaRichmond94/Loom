How does a reader experience a choice point?

When a reader reaches a decision in the story, what happens?

...She stood at the crossroads, the letter still clutched in her hand. The village lights flickered behind her. Whatever she chose now would echo for years.

What does she do?
→ Burn the letter and return home
→ Follow the road north toward the capital

A — Inline Choice

Choices appear directly in the reading flow. Reader picks an option and the story continues seamlessly. Clean, immersive — closest to traditional CYOA books.

...She stood at the crossroads, the letter still clutched in her hand. The village lights flickered behind her. Whatever she chose now would echo for years.

— End of Chapter 3 —
Your choice shapes what comes next
🔥
Burn the letter
🧭
Head north

B — Chapter-End Gate

Story reads normally. At the end of a chapter, a full-screen choice screen appears. More dramatic, lets choices feel like cliffhangers. Common in episodic fiction apps.

Author chooses per scene
Inline choice — small, woven into narrative
Chapter gate — big dramatic moment
Author tags each choice point with its display type in the editor

C — Author Controls Per Choice

Both styles available. When writing, you tag each choice point as "inline" or "chapter gate." Maximally flexible — lets you match the weight of the decision to the moment.
